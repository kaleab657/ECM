EthioCars Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 3, 2026

Welcome to EthioCars, Ethiopia’s premier digital car marketplace. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and securing your personal data. This Privacy Policy strictly conforms to international privacy laws and Google Play Developer Requirements, outlining exactly how we handle your data.

1. What Data is Collected

We only collect information that is strictly necessary for the core functionality of the app.

1.1 Information You Provide

When creating an account or posting a listing, you voluntarily provide:

  • Your real name or display name.
  • Your email address and phone number.
  • Google account basics (if you choose to sign in quickly using Google).
  • The text descriptions and detailed specifications of items you post.
  • The private chat messages you send through our internal communication system.

1.2 Content You Upload

To successfully operate the vehicle marketplace, we collect and store the digital assets you upload, explicitly including photos of the cars you list.

1.3 Automatically Collected Data & Permissions

We automatically collect basic performance diagnostics (such as OS version and device model). Additionally, the app requests specific permissions explicitly needed for functionality:

  • Photos/Media: Required strictly to allow you to upload car images or payment screenshots.
  • Notifications: Required to alert you about incoming chat messages.

2. How Data is Used

Your information is heavily utilized strictly to empower a safe connection between Ethiopian buyers and sellers. We uniquely use your information to:

  • Create and manage your digital account.
  • Display your public car listings to other active users on the marketplace.
  • Continually improve app performance and system reliability.
  • Provide rapid and precise customer support.
  • Proactively monitor, detect, and prevent fraudulent activities or misuse.
  • Ensure a definitively safe, transparent, and secure platform experience.

3. Storage of Data

EthioCars utilizes world-class infrastructure logic powered by Google Cloud (Firebase). We explicitly retain your personal data exclusively for as long as your account remains active. The absolute moment an account is deleted by a user, our active usage retention period ends, and your stored records dictate automatic deletion.

4. Sharing Data

We do not, and will never, arbitrarily sell your personal data. Your information is shared only in meticulously structured scenarios:

  • With Other Users: The contact details and vehicle photos you actively choose to publish on your public car listings are visibly shared with other app users so they can contact you.
  • Service Providers: We leverage critical third-party infrastructure specifically to securely power the app's backbone using Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to reliably track application performance and monitor server health without ever compromising your encrypted personal data.
  • Legal Compliance: Only if required by binding law enforcement mandates.

5. Security of Data

Your passwords are cryptographically hashed, and user data is locked behind highly secure firewalls to ensure maximum protection against unauthorized access. We proactively audit database security rules preventing any internal data exploitation.

6. Processing of Payments/Financial Data

For the absolute safety of our users, EthioCars does not process credit cards, bank accounts, or digital wallets inside the application. To upgrade to a Premium Listing (~600 ETB) or Featured Listing (~300 ETB), you complete the payment safely and externally using highly trusted Ethiopian banking platforms such as CBE (Commercial Bank of Ethiopia) or Telebirr.

Once funds are transferred externally, you upload a screenshot of your digital receipt into the app for admin review and listing activation. We never request, process, or store your actual banking credentials or financial data.

7. User Rights (Account Deletion & Data Removal)

You inherently own your digital footprint. As an EthioCars user, you are permanently empowered with the right to:

  • Access & Update: View and update your personal data and car listings at any point.
  • Account Deletion: You can completely self-terminate your account at any time directly through the app settings. When you trigger the "Delete Account" protocol, your profile data, active car listings, chat message history, and every corresponding photograph you ever uploaded are completely eradicated from our active databases. Deletion is definitive and permanent.
  • Request Data Deletion: You may also request deletion of any specific personal data by contacting us.

8. Cookies

While the native mobile application does not rely heavily on traditional web cookies, certain third-party services (such as Firebase Authentication) may utilize secure tokens and strictly necessary essential cookies to establish and securely maintain your logged-in session across the network.

9. Do Not Track Signals (DNTs)

We absolutely respect user privacy; however, because we do not employ third-party advertising retargeting networks across our native mobile app framework, our systems do not currently respond to automated web-browser "Do Not Track" signals. Your data remains confined solely to the operational necessities of the app.

10. Children's Privacy

EthioCars is a professional vehicle trading platform designed exclusively for adults capable of purchasing automobiles. We completely prohibit usage by children under the age of 13 and do not knowingly collect any data from minors.

11. Changes/Updates to the Privacy Policy

As EthioCars functionally evolves and aligns with Google Play regulations, we may update this highly compliant privacy policy. In the event of material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document so you remain fully informed of your digital rights.
